About this dashboard

Where the data comes from: FiveThirtyEight’s Halloween candy survey. People were shown two candies at a time and picked the one they’d rather get.

Win % is how often a candy won those head-to-head matchups. 50% means it won about half the time.

Reading the box plot: each dot is one candy, and the box shows the middle 50% of candies of that type. “Both” has only one candy (chocolate and fruity), so it’s just a single dot, not a real distribution.

Hover over any dot to see the candy’s name.
